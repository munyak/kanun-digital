# KaNun Digital — Brand Identity Guide

## Brand Essence

**Core Promise:** Enterprise-grade digital security expertise, delivered with the warmth and calm of a wellness brand.

**Brand Attributes:**
- **Calm Authority** — Confident without being aggressive
- **Protective** — Sheltering, not defensive
- **Trustworthy** — Built on 21+ years of real expertise
- **Approachable** — Plain language, no jargon
- **Warm** — Wellness-brand sensibility meets security

**Audience:**
- Families navigating digital challenges
- Parents in custody situations
- Therapists and attorneys making referrals
- Anyone who needs tech help without feeling judged

## Primary Logo: The Minimal Mark

A clean geometric K form housed in a rounded square container with a premium gradient (purple to teal). The abstract K suggests forward motion and clarity. A subtle dot accent adds personality without clutter.

**Why this works:**
- **Modern:** Clean app-icon aesthetic that feels contemporary
- **Scalable:** Strong presence from favicon to billboard
- **Memorable:** Distinctive geometry that's easy to recognize
- **Versatile:** Works on any background with proper color variants
- **Premium:** Refined simplicity that matches the brand positioning

### Mark Variations
- **Full color:** Gradient background (#6B5B95 → #4A9B94) with white K
- **Monochrome:** Solid purple or charcoal background with white K
- **Outline:** Gradient stroke on transparent background (for subtle applications)

### Logo Lockup
The full logo combines the Minimal Mark with the wordmark "KaNun Digital" set in Cormorant Garamond. "KaNun" in charcoal, "Digital" in Calm Purple.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Cormorant Garamond
- Headlines, hero text, pull quotes
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600)
- Conveys: Editorial, premium, calm authority

### Secondary Typeface: Inter
- Body copy, UI elements, captions
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- Conveys: Modern, readable, professional

### Type Scale
- Hero: 72px / 1.08 line-height
- H1: 52px / 1.15
- H2: 36px / 1.2
- H3: 24px / 1.3
- Body: 16px / 1.6
- Small: 14px / 1.5
- Caption: 12px / 1.4

## Logo Usage

### Clear Space
Minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of the mark.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide (full lockup), 32px (mark only)
- Print: 1 inch wide (full lockup), 0.25 inch (mark only)

### Color Variations
1. Full color on light backgrounds
2. Full color on dark backgrounds
3. Monochrome (purple or charcoal)
4. White mark (for dark/photo backgrounds)

### Don'ts
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't add effects (shadows, glows beyond brand system)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Don't rearrange elements
- Don't use unapproved colors

## Voice & Tone

**We are:**
- Calm and reassuring
- Expert but accessible
- Direct but warm
- Confident but not arrogant

**We say:**
- "Digital peace of mind"
- "Practical guidance"
- "Clear steps"
- "We help families thrive"

**We don't say:**
- "Threats" or "dangers" (too fear-based)
- "Solutions" (too corporate)
- "Cyber" anything (too technical)
- "Simple" or "easy" (minimizes their challenges)
