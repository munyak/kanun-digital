★ Final Selection: The Minimal Mark

Concept E: The Minimal Mark has been selected as the primary brand identity.

  • Modern: Clean app-icon aesthetic that feels contemporary and tech-forward
  • Scalable: Strong presence from favicon (32px) to billboard
  • Memorable: Distinctive K geometry that's instantly recognizable
  • Premium: Refined gradient and rounded container match the agency-level website
  • Versatile: Works beautifully on light, dark, and image backgrounds

The dot accent adds subtle personality and balances the composition, making it feel complete without being busy.
