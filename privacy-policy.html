Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

KaNun Digital (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides family-focused digital security consulting services through our website at kanun.digital. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our Site, use our services, or otherwise interact with us.

By using our Site or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Site.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out a form on our Site, schedule a consultation, or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information — your name, email address, and phone number
  • Service interest — which of our services you are inquiring about (e.g., Family Privacy Sweep, Co-Parenting Technology Support, Kids Online Safety, Screen Time Intervention, HIPAA Compliance)
  • Details about your situation — information you voluntarily share in form text fields or during consultations
  • Professional referral information — if you are a therapist, attorney, or mediator contacting us about a professional partnership

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Device and browser information — browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, referring URL
  • IP address — which may indicate your general geographic location
  • Cookies and tracking identifiers — see Section 4 for details

Information From Third-Party Advertising Platforms

We use Google Ads and related Google services to reach families who may benefit from our expertise. Through these services, Google may collect or receive:

  • Cookie and device identifiers
  • IP-derived location data
  • Information about your interactions with our ads
  • Browsing activity on our Site (for conversion tracking and remarketing)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries — processing contact form submissions and scheduling consultations
  • To deliver our services — providing the digital security consulting you have engaged us for
  • To improve our Site — analyzing usage patterns to make our content and user experience better
  • To run and measure advertising — using Google Ads to reach new clients and measure ad effectiveness through conversion tracking
  • To send relevant communications — such as the Family Digital Safety Checklist you requested, or follow-up information related to your inquiry (we do not send unsolicited marketing emails)
  • To comply with legal obligations — such as responding to lawful requests from authorities

3. Google Ads and Remarketing

We use Google Ads, including Google’s remarketing and conversion tracking features, to advertise our services and measure advertising performance. This means:

  • Google Ads conversion tracking — When you click on one of our Google Ads and take an action on our Site (such as submitting a contact form), Google records this as a conversion. This helps us understand which ads are effective.
  • Remarketing — We may show ads to people who have previously visited our Site as they browse other websites in the Google Display Network or use Google Search. These ads are served based on cookies placed during your visit.
  • Google Analytics — We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google Analytics collects data using cookies and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.

How to opt out of personalized advertising:

  • Visit Google’s Ad Settings to control the ads Google shows you
  • Visit the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page to opt out of targeted advertising from participating networks
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies (note: this may affect Site functionality)
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Basic Site functionality (e.g., form submissions) Session
Analytics Understanding how visitors use our Site (Google Analytics) Up to 2 years
Advertising Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing Up to 540 days

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Disabling certain cookies may limit your ability to use some features of our Site.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following categories of third-party services that may receive your information:

  • Google Ads and Google Analytics — advertising and website analytics (see Section 3)
  • Web hosting provider — to serve our Site and process form submissions
  • Email service provider — to deliver communications such as the Digital Safety Checklist

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information with third-party service providers only to the extent necessary for them to perform services on our behalf, and they are contractually required to protect your information.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

  • Contact form submissions — retained for up to 24 months from the date of your inquiry, unless we enter into a service engagement (in which case, retained for the duration of the engagement plus 3 years)
  • Consultation and service records — retained for 7 years following the conclusion of services, as required for professional and legal compliance
  • Analytics data — aggregated and anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely
  • Advertising data — Google Ads data is retained according to Google’s data retention policies

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Privacy Rights

California Residents — CCPA / CPRA Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), provides you with the following rights:

  • Right to Know — You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of that information, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete — You may request that we delete the personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct — You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing — We do not sell your personal information. We may share certain data with Google for targeted advertising purposes, which may constitute “sharing” under the CCPA. You have the right to opt out of this sharing.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise your rights: Contact us at contact@kanun.digital with the subject line “Privacy Rights Request.” We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days, as required by law.

Residents of Other U.S. States

Several other states have enacted comprehensive privacy laws. If you are a resident of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, or another state with applicable privacy legislation, you may have similar rights to access, delete, and correct your personal information. Contact us at contact@kanun.digital to exercise these rights.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our services may involve advising parents and guardians about their children’s online safety, but we do not collect information directly from minors.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us at contact@kanun.digital and we will promptly delete the information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. These measures include encrypted form submissions, secure hosting infrastructure, and access controls limiting who within our organization can view your information.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Links to Other Websites

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as LinkedIn or professional partner sites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your privacy rights, or have concerns about how we handle your information, please contact us:
