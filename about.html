About KaNun Digital

Digital security for families, done calmly.

KaNun Digital helps families get their devices, accounts and screen habits into a shape everyone understands. We bring enterprise security know-how into family life, in plain language and at your family’s pace.

We look at what your family actually has, explain what matters, and help you set it up, without fear tactics.

Who we work with

  • Parents who want a clear picture of their family’s devices, accounts and privacy settings.
  • Separated, co-parenting and blended families who need fair, written rules for shared devices, kids’ apps and photos between two homes.
  • Families with kids and teens who want age-appropriate online safety and healthier screen routines.
  • Therapists, family law attorneys and mediators whose clients need neutral, technical help with family tech questions.
  • Solo and small therapy practices that need hands-on help with HIPAA security.

What we do

  • Family Privacy Sweep — a full review of your family’s devices, accounts and permissions, followed by a plain-language report and a prioritized fix list.
  • Co-Parenting Technology Support — shared-device setup, written digital agreements, and neutral third-party guidance. When a family needs it, we write up our findings clearly and in an organized way.
  • Kids Online Safety — age-appropriate safety education, parental controls setup, and family agreements.
  • Screen Time Intervention — a look at how your family uses screens, then routines and tool settings that fit your household.
  • HIPAA Compliance — practical security work for solo and small therapy practices.

How it works

  1. Assess. We review your family’s devices, accounts, permissions and exposure points.
  2. Educate. We explain the risks and the fixes without the jargon, so everyone knows what matters and why.
  3. Implement. We guide you through setup step by step. You stay in control the whole time.

It starts with a free consultation. We’ll talk about your situation and which service fits. If you decide to go ahead, you get a written agreement with clear pricing for that service before any work begins.
