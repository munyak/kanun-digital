Screen Time Intervention

Break unhealthy patterns and build a family culture of digital balance. We analyze usage, create wellness plans, and provide coaching to help everyone thrive offline and online.

The Problem Is Real

Kids are spending more time on screens than ever. Average screen time for teens has more than doubled in the past decade. And it's not just kids—parents struggle with device addiction too. The devices are designed to be addictive. The algorithms are optimized to keep people engaged. It's not a character flaw; it's by design.

Breaking these patterns takes more than willpower. It takes understanding how devices work, deliberate tools to create friction, and family-wide agreements that support each other. That's where we come in.

What's Included

Usage Analysis

We pull detailed data from devices and accounts to understand actual usage patterns: daily time, peak hours, most-used apps, patterns by device. Data drives strategy.

Wellness Planning

Based on the family's goals and constraints, we design a realistic plan for reducing screen time and building offline activities everyone enjoys.

Tool Configuration

We set up screen time limits, app blockers, downtime schedules, and notification controls—tailored to each family member's needs and age.

Behavioral Coaching

We help families understand why they're reaching for devices, identify triggers, and develop healthier habits. This is about understanding behavior, not just limiting devices.

Family Agreements

We create realistic, agreed-upon family tech agreements. Phones off at dinner. No devices in bedrooms at night. Device-free Sundays. Whatever works for your family.

Progress Tracking

We monitor progress, celebrate wins, troubleshoot obstacles, and adjust the plan as families learn what works and what doesn't.

Does Your Family Need Screen Time Help?

A Screen Time Intervention is right for you if you're experiencing:

  • Kids spending hours daily on screens despite your concerns
  • Conflicts about devices dominating family conversations
  • Kids resisting rules or sneaking device time
  • Impacts on sleep, school, physical activity, or mood
  • Parents feeling addicted to their own devices
  • Want to build healthier device habits but don't know where to start

Our Approach: Understanding, Not Punishment

Screen time issues usually aren't about willpower. Devices are engineered to be addictive. We help families understand the "why" and create sustainable solutions.

Understand the Why

Are kids using screens to escape boredom? Anxiety? FOMO? Social connection? Each cause needs a different solution.

Create Friction

Make unhealthy behaviors harder, healthy behaviors easier. Screen time limits, app blockers, device-free zones, offline activities.

Build Alternatives

Reduced screen time leaves a gap. We help families find activities everyone enjoys: hobbies, time together, physical activity, outdoor time.

How It Works

1

Assessment & Data Gathering

We collect screen time data from devices and apps, interview family members about their habits and concerns, and understand the triggers and patterns.

2

Understanding & Planning

We analyze the data, identify patterns and triggers, and work with the family to set realistic goals and create a phased plan for change.

3

Implementation

We configure tools, set up screen time limits and app blockers, and help the family establish new routines and device-free times.

4

Coaching & Adjustment

We provide ongoing support as the family adjusts. What works? What's too hard? We troubleshoot obstacles and celebrate progress.
