Kids Online Safety

Age-appropriate digital literacy and safety education for your children. We teach smart choices, set up protections, and build family agreements everyone understands.

The Right Approach to Kids & Technology

Technology isn't going away. The goal isn't to shield kids from it, but to teach them to use it wisely. Kids who understand why rules exist follow them better. Kids who know how to recognize danger stay safer online. Kids who help set family agreements buy in to them.

Our approach combines education, practical tools, and family agreements that actually work.

What's Included

Age-Appropriate Safety Education

Tailored conversations for different ages. Preteens learn about privacy and oversharing. Teens learn about digital reputation and online relationships. Every age gets what they're ready for.

Parental Controls Setup

We configure appropriate restrictions on devices and accounts: app limits, content filters, screen time boundaries. We explain how they work so kids understand why.

Digital Citizenship Coaching

How to be a good digital citizen: being kind online, respecting privacy, understanding digital permanence, recognizing and avoiding scams and manipulation.

Family Safety Agreements

We facilitate conversations between parents and kids to create agreed-upon rules. Kids have input. Everyone understands what's expected and why.

Emergency Response Training

What if something goes wrong online? We teach kids and parents how to recognize problems and respond safely: when to tell an adult, what evidence to preserve, who to contact.

Follow-Up Coaching

We remain available to answer questions, adjust agreements as kids grow, and provide ongoing guidance as new platforms and risks emerge.

Tailored to Different Ages

Ages 6-8: Introduction to Digital Tools

Understanding devices, apps, and the internet. Why passwords matter. What personal information is private. How to recognize inappropriate content. Building healthy habits early.

Ages 9-12: Digital Responsibility

Oversharing risks and privacy. Online friendships and predator awareness. Cyberbullying—recognizing it and standing up. Digital footprints and permanence. Managing time online responsibly.

Ages 13-18: Digital Independence

Managing digital reputation. Navigating social media pressure and comparison. Recognizing manipulation and scams. Understanding online relationships. Digital wellbeing and mental health. Preparing for adult digital life.

How It Works

1

Family Assessment

We meet with parents to understand kids' ages, current device usage, concerns, and family values around technology.

2

Age-Appropriate Education

We have separate conversations with kids (age-appropriate) and parents about online safety, building awareness without creating fear.

3

Tools & Setup

We configure parental controls, safety tools, and device restrictions. We show everyone how to use them and why they exist.

4

Family Agreements

We facilitate conversations where kids help set rules. When kids have input, they buy into the agreements more fully.

5

Ongoing Support

As kids age and new platforms emerge, we adjust guidance and agreements. We're here to help navigate changing digital landscapes.
