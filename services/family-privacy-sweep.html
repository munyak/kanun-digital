Service

Family Privacy Sweep

A complete digital security audit that assesses all family devices, accounts, and permissions—then helps you fix vulnerabilities step by step.

What's Included

Device Inventory

Complete review of all family devices, including smartphones, tablets, computers, smart home devices, and wearables. We identify what you have and what each device knows about you.

Account Security Review

Audit of email, social media, financial, and cloud accounts. We check password strength, two-factor authentication, recovery options, and account recovery contacts.

Permission & Privacy Audit

Deep dive into app and system permissions. We identify unnecessary location tracking, camera access, microphone permissions, and data sharing agreements.

Data Exposure Assessment

We check if your family's data has been compromised in known breaches and explain what was exposed and what action to take.

Customized Remediation Plan

Based on our findings, we create a prioritized action plan with clear, step-by-step instructions. We focus on high-impact, practical fixes first.

Implementation Support

We walk you through each step of the remediation plan. You remain in control. We're here to guide, not dictate.

How It Works

1

Initial Consultation

We schedule a 30-minute call to understand your family's digital setup, concerns, and goals. No obligations, just conversation.

2

Comprehensive Assessment

We conduct a thorough audit of devices, accounts, permissions, and privacy settings. This takes 2-3 hours and involves your family's input.

3

Findings Report

We provide a detailed report in plain language. No jargon. You'll understand what we found, why it matters, and how to fix it.

4

Implementation & Support

We guide you through fixes step by step, answer questions, and provide ongoing support as you implement changes.

Who Should Do This

A Family Privacy Sweep is right for you if:

  • You want a complete picture of your family's digital environment
  • You're concerned about privacy but don't know where to start
  • You've experienced a data breach or security incident
  • You're setting up digital rules and boundaries for kids
  • You've just gone through a major family change (separation, blended family, loss of custody)
